**The Cracked Yolk** — a complete original 2D cooking game for the make.fun bounty "Make the best cooking game". You run a tiny diner kitchen: read the order board, build each dish ingredient by ingredient on the plate, and ring the serve bell before the customer's patience runs out. Six original dishes, twelve ingredients, a 3-minute shift, three hearts, and a rush that grows as you serve more.

## How to play

- Read the **order board** (left): each ticket shows a customer, a dish, its ingredients and a patience bar.
- Click an ingredient on the counter — or press its key (`1`–`0`, `Q`, `W`), or move with ← → and press `Space` — to stack it on your plate.
- When the plate matches a ticket exactly, click the golden **SERVE** bell (or press `S`). A wrong dish costs a heart.
- `T` clears the plate. An order whose patience runs out walks away and costs a heart. Lose all 3 hearts, or run out the 3-minute shift, and the shift ends — press `R` to cook again.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # play at http://localhost:8080
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
